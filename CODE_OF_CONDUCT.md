# Code of Conduct

PipeSonata contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

- Be considerate and specific when reviewing work.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.
- Focus disagreement on the work and its observable effects.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates these
expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
